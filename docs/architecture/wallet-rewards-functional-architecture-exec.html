What we offer

Wallet only

A licensed money product: send, receive, cash in and out through agents, airtime and card top-up, treasury and float for the operator, plus fund and withdraw over an external API and merchant cash-in, where a merchant's own wallet funds another customer.

How the money is kept

The design decision everything else rests on

The platform is ledger based. No balance is stored anywhere. A balance is the sum of that account's ledger entries, calculated at the moment it is asked for, which means the books can always be proved from the entries themselves.

No stored balancesNothing is overwritten, so a balance can never silently drift away from the transactions behind it.
One posting pathMoney and points both move as matched pairs of entries through a single posting function, whatever started the transaction.
A lock per accountTransactions hitting the same account are handled one after another under an account lock, so two cannot pass the same check at once.
Checks inside the lockOverdraft and underdraft floors, credit ceilings and the customer's own limits are all evaluated while the lock is held.

Controls that protect the business

Built into the platform, not bolted on later

Two-person approvalNo one person can move operator money or change pricing, limits or conversion rates on their own.
Campaign budgetsEvery campaign has a ceiling, checked before a single point or cent is issued. Marketing cannot overspend.
Anti-drain limitsRedemption is capped per transaction and as a share of balance, so no account can empty the pool at once.
Fails closedA missing price or limit stops the transaction. Nothing runs on an unconfigured default.
